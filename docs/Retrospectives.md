# Sprint Retrospectives

## Sprint 1 Retrospective

### What Went Well

Teamwork: Everyone contributed actively to the sprint’s goals, showcasing strong individual and collective effort.

Communication: The team maintained clear and open lines of communication, ensuring alignment on tasks and priorities.

Meetings: Regular and effective meetings kept the team synchronized and allowed for quick issue resolution.

### What Didn’t Go Well

Planning: Some of the team’s efforts were directed toward issues that were not the highest priority, leading to misallocation of time and effort.

Late Merges: Merge requests were delayed because team members didn't review them in a timely manner, slowing down overall progress.

### Improvements for Next Sprint

Prioritized Planning: Focus planning sessions on the most critical and high-impact features to ensure optimal use of team resources and time.

Streamlined Merge Process: Assign two designated reviewers for each merge request and send them personal notifications to ensure timely reviews.


# Sprint 2 Retrospective

### What Went Well

Teamwork: During this sprint, most team members actively contributed by completing tasks and reviewing others' work.
This demonstrated strong individual efforts and effective collaboration.

### What Didn’t Go Well

Planning: While there was some improvement compared to the last sprint, 
such as focusing on more important features, 
our team still needs to work on setting proper priorities for issues and creating a well-defined plan for the sprint.

Effort Distribution: Several team members contributed insufficient solutions to the issues they picked up
or took significantly longer to complete their tasks.
This under performance by these team members affected the team's overall performance and slowed progress on some issues.

### Improvements for Next Sprint

Visual Progress Tracking: Introduce a visual tool such as a Miro board or mind maps to track progress more effectively.
This will provide a more clear overview of the sprint's progress.

Defined Planning: Focus on creating a more structured and detailed plan before the sprint begins. 
Clearly define what tasks and features are essential, prioritize them effectively,
and ensure all team members understand their responsibilities.
